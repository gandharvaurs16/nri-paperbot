Sources & operating rules

Data is sourced and recorded on each scheduled run. Closing signals are simulated at the next verified opening price. Intraday lows and highs determine stop and target triggers; gaps and daily-bar ambiguity are disclosed. Benchmark return uses dated Nifty closes on matching snapshot dates.
